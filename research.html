Workload-Based Energy Efficiency Analysis of Native and Virtualized Operating Systems for Sustainable Computing: An Experimental Study on Modern Computing Platforms

An experimental comparison of native macOS with Windows and Ubuntu Linux virtual machines on Apple M3 hardware. Four workloads—idle operation, Python execution, video playback, and web browsing—were evaluated using repeated energy measurements and statistical analysis.

Submitted to the IEEE Computer Society Bangladesh Chapter Symposium 2026.

Colorism in Vision-Language Models: A Bangladeshi Study of Skin-Tone and Gender Bias in VLMs

A Bangladesh-focused evaluation of skin-tone and gender-related bias in vision-language models. The study examines model outputs across controlled synthetic identity and socioeconomic scenarios to investigate whether demographic cues influence generated descriptions and decisions.

Robustness and Enhancement Recovery of CNN and Vision Transformer Models for Rice Leaf Disease Classification Under Controlled Image Degradation

A comparative study of MobileNetV3-Small and DeiT-Tiny for seven-class rice leaf disease classification under controlled noise, blur, and low-light conditions. It evaluates gamma correction, CLAHE, and unsharp masking using classification and image-quality metrics.

The experiments show that improved image quality does not necessarily translate into better downstream classification performance.

Empirical Evaluation of Living-off-the-Land (LotL) Attack Techniques and Security Control Limitations in Modern Operating Systems

A cross-platform study of Living-off-the-Land activity on Windows and macOS. The research develops and evaluates two rule-based detection approaches using native-tool activity and contextual features such as execution sequences, timing, process relationships, network activity, and persistence behavior.

The preliminary evaluation uses a catalog of 40 techniques and 200 constructed run-level traces.
