Workload-Based Energy Efficiency Analysis of Native and Virtualized Operating Systems for Sustainable Computing: An Experimental Study on Modern Computing Platforms

Compared native macOS with Windows 11 ARM and Ubuntu 24.04 ARM virtual machines on Apple M3 hardware across four workloads: idle operation, Python execution, video playback, and web browsing. Each workload was evaluated in five 600-second runs using EnergiBridge measurements and statistical analysis.

Key finding: Efficiency varied by workload, with macOS performing best in video and web tasks and the Windows VM showing an advantage in idle and Python tasks. Submitted to the IEEE Computer Society Bangladesh Chapter Symposium 2026.

Colorism in Vision-Language Models: A Bangladeshi Study of Skin-Tone and Gender Bias in VLMs

Evaluated four vision-language models for skin-tone and gender bias using 100 synthetic Bangladeshi facial images (50 identity pairs), 1,200 socioeconomic profiles, and 4,000 simulated recruitment decisions.

Key findings: Gemini's light-skin profiles received BDT 8,877 higher estimated income (p < 0.001); light-skin selection rates were 66.87% (Gemini), 66.35% (GPT), and 59.76% (DeepSeek). Llama showed candidate-position confounding across 1,000 fixed-seed trials.

Robustness and Enhancement Recovery of CNN and Vision Transformer Models for Rice Leaf Disease Classification Under Controlled Image Degradation

Benchmarked MobileNetV3-Small (71.08% accuracy) and DeiT-Tiny (74.51% accuracy) for seven-class rice leaf disease classification under controlled noise, blur, and low-light degradation.

Key finding: Gamma correction improved MobileNetV3-Small accuracy by 8.33 percentage points under low light. Evaluation used accuracy, macro-F1, PSNR, and SSIM; better visual quality did not consistently improve classification.

Empirical Evaluation of Living-off-the-Land (LotL) Attack Techniques and Security Control Limitations in Modern Operating Systems

A cross-platform study of Living-off-the-Land (LotL) activity on Windows and macOS, developing and evaluating two rule-based detection approaches using native-tool activity and context such as execution sequences, timing, process relationships, network activity, and persistence behavior.

Evaluation scope: A catalog of 40 techniques and 200 synthetically constructed traces.
