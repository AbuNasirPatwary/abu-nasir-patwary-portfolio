Full-stack web platform

Khabo-Koi

Restaurant Discovery & Table Reservation Platform

A platform for customers to discover and book restaurants, restaurant and branch managers to oversee dining operations, and platform administrators to manage the service. React.js and Tailwind CSS deliver the interface, Django REST Framework handles APIs and booking logic, and PostgreSQL stores application data.

  • Restaurant discovery: Customers explore restaurants, branches, menus, and table availability before booking.
  • Reservations and ordering: Transaction-safe table reservations, food pre-orders, and a simulated advance-payment workflow.
  • AI and management: An AI dining assistant and four role-based dashboards for customers, restaurant managers, branch managers, and platform administrators.
  • Technical scale: 51 REST API routes and 192 automated backend test methods.

University community platform

CampusLink

University Community Management Platform

A campus platform where students and alumni share resources, participate in campus activities, and connect with their community, while administrators review accounts and moderate content. React.js and Tailwind CSS power the interface, Spring Boot provides APIs and business logic, and PostgreSQL stores application data.

  • Four community modules: FindIt (lost and found), BorrowHub (borrowing requests), Campus Complaints, and Talent Sharing.
  • Community interaction: Students and alumni create posts and requests, comment, react, and receive notifications.
  • Administration and security: JWT authentication, role-based access, alumni verification, and content moderation.
  • Technical scale: 62 HTTP handlers, 15 JPA entities, and asynchronous activity logging.
